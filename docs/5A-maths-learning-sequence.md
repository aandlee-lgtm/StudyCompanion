# 5A. Confirmed Mathematics learning sequence (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced)

Source of sequence: *CambridgeMATHS NSW Stage 6 Mathematics Advanced Year 12, Second Edition*.
The full list of 64 subtopics (1A–10F) is in `maths-advanced-y12-sequence.json`.

## Authority
- The Cambridge sequence decides **when** material is introduced.
- The official NESA syllabus decides **what** is examinable. Where the two differ, NESA wins.

## Status tracking
- Status is tracked per **subtopic**, not just per chapter.
- Statuses: `LOCKED`, `LEARNING`, `LEARNED`, `REVISION`.
- Default for a student starting Year 12: 1A = `LEARNING`, every later subtopic = `LOCKED`.
- The student can change any status straight away.

## Daily question mix (starting rules, adapt to performance)
| Source | Share |
|---|---|
| Current `LEARNING` subtopic(s) | 50–60 % |
| Recently `LEARNED` material | 20–30 % |
| Older revision / Year 11 assumed knowledge | 10–20 % |

- `LOCKED` subtopics never appear in an ordinary daily session.
- When a new subtopic becomes `LEARNING`: keep revising earlier subtopics, gradually lower the
  weight of older mastered material, and raise the weight of weak areas.

## Enrichment
- 3D (Challenge — proving the fundamental theorem) and 6F (Challenge — the derivative of sin x is
  cos x) are tagged `ENRICHMENT` by default.
- They get no normal daily weighting unless NESA explicitly requires the content, or the student
  turns on challenge/enrichment practice.
