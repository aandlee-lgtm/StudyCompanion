HSC Companion 2027 — installing on a Mac

1. Drag "HSC Companion" onto the Applications folder.
2. The first time, open Applications, right-click (or Control-click) HSC Companion and choose Open,
   then click Open again. (The app is not notarised by Apple, so macOS asks once. If it still
   refuses: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".)
3. A small window says the app is running, and Chrome opens at http://127.0.0.1:8000.
   Keep the small window open while you study; closing it stops the app.

A safety copy of your data is made automatically each day you open the app (the last 14 are
kept). You can also export a backup any time from Settings — keep one outside your Mac too.

Your data (answers, progress, settings, backups) lives in
~/Library/Application Support/HSC Companion — it is kept when you update or delete the app.
To use AI marking, edit the .env file in that folder (the app's Settings page shows the path)
and restart the app.
